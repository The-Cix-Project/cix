## Official Cix artifacts

An official Cix release, package, installer image or recipe catalogue is one
signed with a key The Cix Project publishes for that purpose (the release and
catalogue keys in the Cix repository's `docs/keys/`) and distributed from the
project's own channels. Anyone may build and redistribute the source under its
licence. That does not make what they build an official Cix artifact, and
nothing they build carries the project's signature.

website, and its updates and support are not granted by any software licence.
The project provides them at its discretion.
